Nova Chatbot

Meet Nova, your on-device chatbot

Nova understands ~25 intents using TF-IDF similarity, can do math, tell jokes, remember your name for the session, and shows how confident it is in every reply. No server, no account.

Honest limits: Nova is a classic intent-matching chatbot, not a large language model. It answers what it was trained for and says "I'm not sure" instead of making things up.
